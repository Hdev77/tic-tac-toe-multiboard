# Tic Tac Toe – AI Challenge

An interactive Tic Tac Toe game where you can challenge an AI opponent across multiple difficulty levels and board sizes.

Choose your preferred challenge, test your strategy, and see if you can beat the AI!

## ✨ Features

- **AI Opponent:** Play against an AI-powered opponent.
- **3 Difficulty Levels:** Choose between Easy, Medium, and Hard.
- **Multiple Board Sizes:** Play on 3×3, 4×4, or 5×5 grids.
- **Interactive Gameplay:** Enjoy a smooth and engaging gaming experience.
- **Dynamic Game Modes:** Customize your game by selecting your preferred difficulty and board size.

## 🎮 Game Modes

| Board Size |  Difficulty Levels   |
| :--------: | :------------------: |
|    3×3     | Easy · Medium · Hard |
|    4×4     | Easy · Medium · Hard |
|    5×5     | Easy · Medium · Hard |

Take turns placing your marks on the board and try to achieve the winning combination before your AI opponent does.

Can you master all three difficulty levels?
